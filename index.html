How it works

Simple. Focused. Effective.

The Eisenhower Matrix was used by US President Dwight D. Eisenhower to manage the most demanding job in the world. Now it's yours — in seconds.

01
Add your tasks

Type anything on your mind — work tasks, errands, ideas. No complex setup or formatting required.

02
Rate urgency & importance

Two simple dropdowns automatically place each task into the correct quadrant of the matrix.

03
See what matters now

The matrix instantly shows you what to do today, what to schedule, what to delegate, and what to cut entirely.

04
Check off & move on

Mark tasks done as you go. Export a CSV report anytime for weekly reviews or sharing with your team.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Built for people who actually want to get things done — not spend hours managing a productivity system.

🔥
Focus mode

A persistent banner always surfaces your single most critical task so you never lose sight of what's truly urgent and important right now.

📊
Live task stats

See your full task breakdown at a glance — pending vs done, counts per quadrant. Real accountability made visual.

🔄
Matrix + List views

Toggle between the 2×2 matrix for the big picture or a clean table view when you need to scan and sort everything quickly.

⚡
Effort tagging

Tag each task low, medium, or high effort so you can batch similar tasks together or pick quick wins when energy is low.

💾
Auto-save

Everything is saved to your browser automatically. Close the tab, reopen tomorrow — your tasks are exactly where you left them.

📥
CSV export

Download your full task list as a spreadsheet anytime. Perfect for weekly reviews, status reports, or sharing with your team.
